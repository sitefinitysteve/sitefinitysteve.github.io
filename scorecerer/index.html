The Wizard scorepad

Scorecerer

Bid · Score · Roast

Keep score. Talk trash.

A scorepad for the card game Wizard: bid tricks, score each round, and roast the table.

  • Tap in bids and tricks round by round. The app does the math.
  • Every round ends with a roast for whoever blew their bid.
  • Your games stay on your phone. No accounts, no sign-in.
